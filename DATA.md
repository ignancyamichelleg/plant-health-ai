\# Dataset



\- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI-Lab-Makerere/beans (Makerere AI Lab)

\- Licence: MIT

\- Classes: angular\_leaf\_spot, bean\_rust, healthy

\- Splits: train 1,034 / validation 133 / test 128 (1,295 images)

\- Format: 500x500 RGB JPEG

\- Download: `python -m plant\_health.data.download`

&#x20; (saved to `data/raw/<split>/<label>/`; images are re-saved as JPEG quality 95)
